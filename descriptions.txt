Living Room
The small living room contains a couch, coffee table, and two armchairs.
Pictures of various cousins adorn the walls along with two of your aunt's wretched attempts at oil painting.
You can't help but notice that the carpet could use a good vacuuming.
Doorways lead west to your father's office, north to your bedroom, and east to the kitchen.
#
Bedroom
This is your domain and has been since your family moved here when you were six.
A nightstand with a Superman lamp sits next to your twin bed.
Posters of various anime characters cover one wall.
A pile of laundry, slowly but surely attaining sentence, occupies the far corner.
The door to the attic is in the ceiling above the pile of clothes.
You can return south to the living room.
#
Office
This is the place where your father spends most of his time.
You see an old work laptop, a bookshelf with a bunch of dusty books, and a toolbox.
I wonder if anything in here can help get the key?
From the office door you can go east to the living room.
#
Kitchen
The fridge makes a rumbling noise, making you wonder if there are any good snacks to grab?
You see the pizza boxes from earlier that you and your family were eating.
The garage door to the south just won't seem to budge.
The only place you can go to is west, to the living room.
#
Attic
You can't remember the last time you've actually used this thing.
There are all kinds of old toys and games just sitting here.
You even find old wedding photos of your parents.
The door leading downwards returns you to your bedroom.
#
Garage
The car, the car is here!
Dad doesn't want me to drive it, but I just can't resist.
There's a whole bunch of boxes also just sitting here, probably stuff for repairs.
The door here takes you north, back to the kitchen.
